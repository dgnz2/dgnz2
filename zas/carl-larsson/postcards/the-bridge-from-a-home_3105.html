The Bridge (From A Home) - Carl Larsson Fine Art Postcard

The painting The Bridge with Pig brims with summertime activity. A small wooden bridge dominates the foreground, casting a cool shadow over the sparkling stream below. A horse-drawn carriage piled high with hay crosses the bridge, the brown horse straining slightly under the load. To its right, a surprised pig dashes across the bridge, its pink form adding a touch of humor to the scene. In the background, a red-roofed farmhouse with white trim basks in the sun. Smoke curls from the chimney, hinting at life within. Laundry flutters on a line, and lush green meadows stretch towards the horizon. The scene is bathed in warm sunlight, capturing the vibrancy of a summer day.
