When Children Have Gone To Bed (From A Home) - Carl Larsson Fine Art Postcard

The painting depicts a cozy domestic scene lit by a lamp on the table. A man and a woman sit facing each other at the table. The man, dressed in a suit and tie, leans back in his chair with a contented expression. The woman, wearing a long dress, smiles at the man while holding a sewing project in her lap. A red patterned rug is spread beneath the table, and a vase with flowers sits near the lamp in the center of the table. On the wall behind the woman is a large framed picture. The overall mood of the painting is one of peace and intimacy.
