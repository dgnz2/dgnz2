Danae 1907 - Gustav Klimt Signature Poster

The painting depicts a lone figure – a woman named Danaë – sprawled on a plush, patterned bed. Her body is curled suggestively, one arm draped over her head, the other reaching out. A deep purple, almost black, veil with swirling gold designs pools around her, emphasizing her pale skin. But the most striking element is the golden rain that fills the upper half of the canvas. It cascades down in a mesmerizing flow, a stark contrast to the flat, decorative patterns that dominate the background. This shimmering gold is not just rain; it represents Zeus, the king of the gods, who transformed himself to seduce Danaë.
