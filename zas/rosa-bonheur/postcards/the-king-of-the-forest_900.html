The King Of The Forest - Rosa Bonheur Fine Art Postcard

The painting "The King of the Forest" by Rosa Bonheur, and it features a majestic stag standing alert in a dense forest. The stag is the central focus of the painting. It's a large red deer with a powerful body and an impressive rack of antlers. The antlers are multi-pointed and curving forward. The stag's head is held high and it looks out from the canvas with a watchful expression. The background shows a dense forest with towering trees and a dark and shadowy undergrowth. Sunlight filters through the leaves in a few places, creating patches of light on the forest floor. The overall impression of the painting is one of power and majesty. The stag stands out as the dominant creature in its forest domain.
